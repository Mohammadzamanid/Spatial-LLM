# Nature Machine Intelligence submission checklist

Target content type: **Article**

Current official Article constraints checked 7 October 2026:
- Main text: <=3,500 words excluding abstract, Methods, references and legends.
- Abstract: <=150 words, unreferenced.
- Display items: <=6.
- Structure: unheaded Introduction; Results; Discussion; Methods.
- Numerical references required at final-format stage.
- Initial submission may be PDF, Word or TeX/LaTeX and does not require house-style formatting.
- Cover letter required.
- Supplementary Information optional.
- LLM use must be disclosed in Methods.
