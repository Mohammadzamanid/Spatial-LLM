# Figure legends

## Figure 1 | Causal language transfer through the cognitive-map channel

**a,** System schematic. Self-motion is integrated into a latent cognitive-map state and injected into a frozen language model through gated spatial-to-text fusion; the move sequence is not included in the prompt. **b,** Leakage-controlled torus readout with cortex ON versus text-only OFF at path lengths 8, 16 and 24. Bars show mean exact accuracy across six seeds; error bars are 95% confidence intervals. ON exceeds OFF at every length in all six seeds (paired sign-flip p=0.0328 at each length). **c,** Additional language readouts through latent state: elapsed-time exact classification, allocentric WHERE, head-direction FACING and egocentric LANDMARK. Bars show reported multi-seed means for cortex ON and OFF. **d,** Organ-specific lesion controls. For each readout, ablating the organ carrying the relevant subcode selectively reduces accuracy, whereas ablating the comparison organ largely spares the readout. WHERE/FACING use n=6 seeds; allocentric WHERE/LANDMARK use n=8 seeds. Exact statistics and seed-level artifacts are in `results/elapsed_time_llm.json`, `deadreckoning_llm_agg.json`, `multiframe_llm_agg.json` and `torus_llm.json`.

## Figure 2 | The value of structured spatial coding is regime-dependent

**a,** Certified null on ordinary Euclidean path integration: grid and a permutation-invariant NoPE+sum Transformer have nearly identical distance accuracy at T=24 (0.9274 versus 0.9257; paired mean difference +0.0017, 95% CI [-0.0221,+0.0317], permutation p=0.9384, Cohen's d=0.042; n=8). **b,** Against a bounded place code, the grid representation retains more distance information at T=24 (0.9237 versus 0.7996; n=20, paired mean difference +0.1241, 95% CI [+0.1194,+0.1290]). **c,** Regime map assembled from committed multi-seed experiments. Cell values are task metrics; annotations summarize whether a representation wins, loses or ties within each tested regime. The panel is descriptive synthesis rather than an additional statistical test. Source artifacts: `results/significance.json` and `results/phase_diagram.json`.

## Figure 3 | Periodicity and remapping become useful when their structure is load-bearing

**a,** Toroidal path integration. Mean fraction of predictions within 45° across eight seeds is shown as path length increases. The periodic grid representation remains at 1.0, while NoPE+sum and Euclidean place representations collapse toward chance after repeated wraps. Error bars in the underlying artifact are 95% confidence intervals. **b,** Context-free one-shot multimap memory (n=5 seeds). Remapped grid and place populations maintain high recall as the number of contexts increases, whereas the same grid without remapping and a deterministic additive displacement code suffer cross-context collisions. Lines show means; error bars are 95% confidence intervals. **c,** Trained classifier with an explicit context label (n=5 seeds). Once room identity is externally supplied, non-remapping grid and additive codes remain highly accurate, showing that an external label can substitute for internal remapping. Source artifacts: `results/torus.json`, `results/code_necessity.json` and `results/multimap_task.json`.

## Figure 4 | Prospective theta-sweep information is causal and replicates across LLM families

**a,** Qwen2.5-1.5B blocked-ahead task in novel layouts (n=8 seeds). Bars show mean accuracy +/- 95% CI for intact ON, text-only OFF, NO-SWEEP and wrong-heading sweep conditions; chance is 0.50. ON exceeds every ablation in all eight seeds; paired sign-flip p=0.0081 for each contrast. **b,** SmolLM2-1.7B replication (n=8 seeds) using forced-choice 0/1 scoring. Mean ON accuracy is 0.699 versus 0.498 for NO-SWEEP, 0.500 for OFF and 0.555 for wrong-heading sweep. **c,** Per-seed SmolLM2 ON-minus-NO-SWEEP effects. Seven seeds are positive, one is tied and none is negative; exact paired sign-flip p=0.015625. Source artifacts: `results/theta_sweep_llm_agg.json` and `results/sweep_llm_smollm2_v2.json`.

## Figure 5 | External validation on real human GeoLife trajectories

**a,** Locked external-validation protocol. Microsoft GeoLife GPS trajectories are converted to east/north displacement windows and assigned to seeded-random user-disjoint 70/15/15 train/validation/test partitions. Spatial scaling and distance-bin thresholds are estimated from training users only before a fixed grid population is decoded. **b,** Test-set accuracy across eight readout seeds. GRID reaches 0.7655 on 8-way bearing and 0.9234 on 6-bin distance; a corrected exact-displacement RAW-MLP calibration reaches 0.9866 and 0.9879, respectively; train-majority OFF baselines are 0.1616 and 0.1838. **c,** Held-out-user paired GRID-minus-OFF effects across 28 test users. Points are mean user-level effects and error bars are 95% user-bootstrap confidence intervals: bearing +0.593 [0.505,0.675], distance +0.765 [0.725,0.803]. User-level sign-flip p is approximately 1e-5 for both tasks. Source artifact: `results/geolife_external_v2.json`.
